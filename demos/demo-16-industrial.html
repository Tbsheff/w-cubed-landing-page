Our Story

Built on 38 Years of Trust

W-Cubed was founded in 1986 by a veteran who believed water infrastructure deserved the same discipline and precision he learned in service. Starting with custom control panels built in a garage, we've grown into the Mountain West's go-to water equipment representative.

Today we represent 10+ leading manufacturers and serve municipalities, engineers, and industrial facilities across four states. Our success is rooted in technical expertise, reliable service, and genuine long-term partnerships.
