Our Story

A Legacy of Precision & Integrity

Founded in 1986, W-Cubed has grown from a small regional contractor into one of the Mountain West's most trusted water and wastewater infrastructure providers. Our veteran-led team brings military discipline and precision to every project.

We believe that clean water infrastructure is the foundation of healthy communities. Every system we design, build, and maintain reflects that commitment to public health and environmental stewardship.
